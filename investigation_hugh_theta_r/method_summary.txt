Short method summary
1. Start from all tumbling-rod recordings in the dataset.
2. Some filters applied to keep only recordings where the rod doesn't leave the fov
3. Pool all accepted X,Y points together, ignoring which recording they came from.
4. Convert each X,Y point to phi and r.
5. Bin points by phi from 0 to 180 degrees in 5 degree bins.
6. Sample N points from every phi bin. So we are the selected points form a uniform phi distribution.
7. Plot r density histogram for sampled points. Fit theta(r) so that cos(theta) densithy plot is uniform (equal occupation of all solid angles. 
8. Estimate uncertainty by recording-level bootstrap: resample whole recordings with replacement, repeat the full pooling, phi-binning, balanced sampling, and theta(r) fitting, then take the 16th and 84th percentiles as the 1 sigma bounds.

Parameters used
- Phi bin width: 5 degrees
- Recording filter: range_x > 1 and range_y > 1
- Balanced sample rule: half of the least populated phi-bin count
- Bootstrap type: recording-level bootstrap
- Bootstrap replicates: 250
